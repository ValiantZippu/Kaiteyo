## 🔄 Upgrading

- **Windows**: run the new EXE — your data is preserved automatically.
- **macOS**: drag the new app over the old one in Applications.
- **Linux**: use your package manager / swap the AppImage.
- Never uninstall before upgrading; Kaiteyo keeps your study data in its own
  data directory.
