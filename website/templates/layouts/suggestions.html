Suggestions

Propose a plan for Kaiteyo — a feature, a fix, a refactor, a research question, even an architecture change. The official plan is protected: your proposal never edits the roadmap directly. A maintainer reviews it, and if it is accepted it becomes an official roadmap or kanban item, with your original proposal preserved as provenance.

Proposal template

A good proposal answers every field below — do not file “Feature request: add X.” The more context, the faster the triage.

  1. Title — one line that names the outcome.
  2. Problem — what is missing or broken, with a real scenario.
  3. Proposed solution — what you would build.
  4. Why it matters — who benefits and how.
  5. Affected systems — e.g. Dictionary, Media, Mining, Game.
  6. Possible implementation — rough approach (no code required).
  7. Dependencies — what it needs to exist first.
  8. Potential risks — what could go wrong.
  9. Alternatives — what else was considered.
  10. Screenshots / diagrams — if they help.
  11. Related documentation — links to existing docs.
  12. Priority suggestion — P0 critical / P1 high / P2 medium / P3 low.
