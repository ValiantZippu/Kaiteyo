## Origins

Kaiteyo is the open-source continuation of [Kanji Dojo](https://github.com/syt0r/Kanji-Dojo) by **syt0r** (Yaroslav Shuliak, © 2022–2023) — rebuilt in Kotlin Multiplatform with its own design language, roadmap, and feature set.

## Data & resources

- **KanjiVG** — writing strokes and radical information (CC BY-SA 3.0)
- **JMDict** — Japanese–multilingual dictionary (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- **JmdictFurigana** — furigana resource (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- **Leeds University Frequency List** — word frequency ranking (CC BY)

## Technology

- **Kotlin Multiplatform & Compose Multiplatform** — the entire app, one codebase for six platforms
- **Noto fonts** — consistent, beautiful CJK glyph rendering
- **GitHub** — hosting, releases, and issue tracking
