## First launch

Once installed, Kaiteyo takes you through first-launch setup — choose your base mode and accent, and you're ready to create your first deck. The [getting started guide](/docs/user-guide/getting_started/) walks through it step by step.

## Data lives on your device

Kaiteyo is offline-first. Study data, decks and progress are stored locally; no account is required to use the app. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) and [security](/security/) pages for details.
