## Get involved

- **Report bugs** — open an issue with the `bug` label; include your platform, version and steps to reproduce.
- **Request features** — check the [roadmap](/roadmap/) first, then open a discussion.
- **Contribute code** — the [contributing guide](/contributing/) covers development setup, coding standards and the contribution workflow.
- **Security** — report vulnerabilities privately via the [security page](/security/).

## Upstream

Kaiteyo is the open-source continuation of [Kanji Dojo](https://github.com/syt0r/Kanji-Dojo), rebuilt in Kotlin Multiplatform with a unified design across all platforms.
