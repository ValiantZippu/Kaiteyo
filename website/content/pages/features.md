## Study

<div class="feature-grid">
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-icon"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-grid"/></svg></div>
    <h3>Deck management</h3>
    <p>Organize study material into decks and presets. Reorder, rename, and edit cards freely — your data never leaves your device.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-icon"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-flip"/></svg></div>
    <h3>Learn new material</h3>
    <p>Introduce new cards with card flipping, typing, and multiple-choice recognition modes.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-icon"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-review"/></svg></div>
    <h3>Reviews</h3>
    <p>Grade cards with Again / Hard / Good / Easy. SRS scheduling adapts per card, per deck, with configurable intervals.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-icon"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-pen"/></svg></div>
    <h3>Stroke-order writing</h3>
    <p>Practice handwriting kanji with real-time stroke grading. Drawn kanji are recognized and matched against the answer.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Built-in tools

<div class="feature-grid">
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-icon"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-book"/></svg></div>
    <h3>Dictionary</h3>
    <p>17,000+ vocabulary entries with meanings and example sentences, plus Yomitan-format imports so you can add your own dictionaries and search them side by side.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-icon"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-play"/></svg></div>
    <h3>Media workspace</h3>
    <p>Open video, audio, subtitles, images, PDFs, and text. Bookmark timestamps, capture audio clips, and mine sentences straight from subtitles.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-icon"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-zap"/></svg></div>
    <h3>Mining</h3>
    <p>Turn dictionary entries, subtitle lines, browser selections, and OCR captures into review cards with the context sentence intact.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-icon"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-eye"/></svg></div>
    <h3>OCR & screen capture</h3>
    <p>Recognize Japanese text from images, the clipboard, or your screen — then look it up or mine it. Tesseract-powered with graceful fallback.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-icon"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-monitor"/></svg></div>
    <h3>Learning browser</h3>
    <p>A lightweight browser with tabs, reader mode, and bookmarks — read the web and mine vocabulary while you go.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-icon"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-font"/></svg></div>
    <h3>Fonts & reading support</h3>
    <p>Noto fonts bundled for consistent kanji rendering. Custom font import keeps the app readable with your favorites.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-icon"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-sync"/></svg></div>
    <h3>Export & backup</h3>
    <p>Export decks to share or back up your study data. Import brings decks back on any device.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <div class="feature-icon"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true"><use href="#icon-settings"/></svg></div>
    <h3>Deep customization</h3>
    <p>Accents, fonts, themes, review intervals, keyboard shortcuts, and more — tuned the way you study.</p>
  </div>
</div>
