## Themes at a glance

Try the live theme controls on the [Theme Gallery](/theme-gallery/) page — every screenshot here was taken with the default OLED theme and signature accent.

Desktop builds share the exact same UI: the sidebar, deck browser, and study screen scale from a phone to a full-screen desktop window without losing a single feature.

## Desktop

The desktop window shell — custom title bar, launcher, and launchpad — is captured with
`scripts/capture-window-shell.sh` (the app's dev-only `--capture-state=` mode) and stored in
`docs/screenshots/`; the site builder copies those images here on every build, so the gallery
always matches the repository.
