Kaiteyo is a complete Japanese-learning environment. This page is the deep version of the homepage story — how each capability works and how they connect.

## The problem Kaiteyo solves

Japanese learning tools are scattered. A flashcard app for review, a dictionary for lookups, a media player for immersion, a writing app for kanji — none of them know what you've already studied. Kaiteyo replaces the scatter with one environment: encounter, look up, mine, study, write, measure. The same content flows through every stage.

## Learning

- **Kanji** — readings, meanings, radicals, stroke count, frequency and JLPT level from open datasets (KANJIDIC, KanjiVG), resolved automatically when you add a character.
- **Vocabulary** — JMdict-backed entries with readings, furigana, part of speech and examples; each word links to the kanji it contains.
- **Kana** — katakana and hiragana study with the same writing and review pipeline as kanji.
- **SRS** — a real scheduler with new / learning / review / relearning states and calibrated intervals. Card-by-card review history drives every screen.

## Writing

Writing practice is a first-class study mode, not a toy:

- Stroke-order reference from KanjiVG
- Per-stroke feedback on order, direction, start/end position and shape
- Configurable tolerances — the evaluator is strict enough to teach, forgiving enough to not frustrate
- Writing accuracy feeds statistics, deck progress and exam recommendations

## Decks & Library

- Real deck entities: create, rename, merge, archive, delete, nest, pin favorites
- JLPT decks (N5–N1), custom decks, mined decks
- Live counts — new, learning, due, mature — derived from the same SRS state Study and Home use
- Powerful search: expression, reading, meaning, JLPT, frequency, radical, stroke count, tags

## Media & Mining

- **Media Centre** — local video playback with Japanese subtitles
- Select subtitle text, look it up in the built-in dictionary, mine it into a deck with sentence, screenshot and source timestamp
- Mined cards are normal Kaiteyo notes — they flow into SRS, statistics and exams like any other card

## Anki integration

Kaiteyo stands on its own — the database, scheduler, reviews and statistics are Kaiteyo's own. Anki integration is optional: send mined cards to Anki alongside Kaiteyo, or not at all. No lock-in in either direction.

## Statistics & Exams

- One authoritative statistics system: study time, reviews, retention, writing accuracy, deck progress, JLPT progress, mining activity — with an activity heatmap
- Exams generated from your actual studied content — kanji recognition, reading, vocabulary, meaning, writing, mixed

## Customization

OLED black, dark, light and sepia base modes, seven signature accents, configurable animation speed, and a theme studio. The same design language from phones to desktops, light or dark.

## Platform

Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose — one codebase for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS. Offline-first: all data lives on your device.
