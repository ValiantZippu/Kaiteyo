## In short

Kaiteyo stores **everything on your device**. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** Kaiteyo has no telemetry, no crash reporting services, and no advertising. Your decks, cards, review history, and settings live in local files on your machine.

## What the website collects

This website is fully static — it is served as files, with no server-side logging beyond standard hosting access logs. Your theme and navigation preferences are stored in your browser's `localStorage` and never leave your browser.

## Third-party data

The dictionary data bundled with the app comes from the sources listed on the [Credits](/credits/) page. These are offline datasets shipped with the app — they do not communicate with any server.

## When you choose to share

If you report a bug, join a Discussion, or open a pull request on GitHub, you share whatever you choose to include there — following GitHub's privacy practices. We only use reported data to fix issues and improve the app.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the project evolves. Material changes will be announced in the [Changelog](/changelog/) and on GitHub.
