## Themes in the app

Every choice below is persisted the same way the app persists yours — the site remembers your base mode, accent, and glass setting across visits, and applies it instantly on every page.

The design system maps 1:1 to the app: the signature accent is lime `#C2FC8B` with orange `#FEAB57` as secondary, and the OLED base mode uses true black `#050505` so pixels literally turn off.
