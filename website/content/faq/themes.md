---
category: Customization
title: Can I customize how Kaiteyo looks?
---

Yes. Kaiteyo ships base modes (OLED black, dark, light, and sepia reading mode) with seven signature accent schemes, plus configurable animation speed and display density. On desktop there's a full Theme Studio — color editor, gradients, motion presets, layout (density/radius/glow) — with theme import/export as JSON. Your choices persist locally and carry across visits. See the [theme documentation](/docs/features/themes/) and the [Theme Gallery](/theme-gallery/).
