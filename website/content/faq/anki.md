category: Study & review
title: Can I use Kaiteyo with Anki?
---

Yes, in two real ways. **`.apkg` import/export** works on every platform — bring an Anki collection into Kaiteyo (deck hierarchy, notes, cards, tags and scheduling carry over; media is extracted and references repaired) or export Kaiteyo decks to Anki. **AnkiConnect** (desktop) is a live bridge to a running local Anki: push mined cards straight into Anki, or import whole decks over Anki's localhost API.

Kaiteyo is not Anki — it has its own scheduler (FSRS-5) and statistics. Integration is optional and additive, never a dependency. Exact details and honest compatibility limits are in the [Anki documentation](/docs/integrations/anki/).
