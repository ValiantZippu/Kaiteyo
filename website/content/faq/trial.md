---
category: Getting started
title: What is the web trial, and what does it store?
---

The [web trial](/try/) is a working slice of Kaiteyo that runs entirely in your browser — study flashcards, browse kanji and vocabulary, practice writing strokes, and see progress. It uses a small sample dataset (a taste of the bundled N5 data), and every interaction is stored in your browser's `localStorage`. Nothing is sent to any server, and clearing your browser data removes it completely. The trial deliberately omits desktop-only features like the Media Centre, mining, and local file access.
