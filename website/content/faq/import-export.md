category: Data & privacy
title: What formats can Kaiteyo import and export?
---

Kaiteyo imports and exports **JSON, CSV, TSV and TXT** decks, plus **Anki `.apkg`** packages on every platform — with preview, validation, and conflict policies (Skip / Update / Duplicate). Profile **backups** archive your data, settings, and window state into a single restore-able archive. On desktop, user dictionaries can be imported in Yomitan-compatible formats (ZIP/JSON, JMdict/KANJIDIC/KanjiVG). See the [backup FAQ](/faq/#faq-backup) and the [import/export documentation](/docs/user-guide/getting_started/).
