category: Study & review
title: How do exams work, and how are they different from reviews?
---

Exams are generated from your **actually studied** content — kanji you've studied (with radicals/stroke counts) and vocabulary from your decks — with JLPT distribution, production (free-text) and recognition (multiple-choice) question types, valid distractors, and a reproducible seed. A **weekly exam** covers the trailing 7 days automatically. Exams measure knowledge without the usual flashcard affordances: no hints, strict writing grading, and a countdown. Wrong answers become learning-mistake records that feed your weakness analytics, and you can review every question afterwards. See the [statistics documentation](/docs/features/statistics/).
