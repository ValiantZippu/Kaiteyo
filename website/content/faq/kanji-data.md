title: Where does Kaiteyo's kanji and vocabulary data come from?
---

From openly licensed datasets, processed by Kaiteyo's own data pipeline (KJD): **KanjiVG** for stroke paths and radicals (CC BY-SA 3.0), **KANJIDIC** for kanji meanings/readings/classifications, **JMdict** for vocabulary entries, **JmdictFurigana** for reading segmentation, **Tanos JLPT lists** for level classification, and frequency data from the **Leeds** corpus. Everything is bundled offline — no runtime downloads, no phone-home. Full attribution and licenses are on the [Data & Attribution](/data/) page.
