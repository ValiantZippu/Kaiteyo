category: Media & mining
title: How does media mining work, and on which platforms?
---

The Media Centre (desktop) plays local video and audio through VLC, mpv, or the built-in audio engine. With Japanese subtitles (SRT/ASS/SSA/VTT), you select subtitle text, look it up in the built-in dictionary, and mine it into a deck — the card keeps the sentence, reading, definition, a screenshot of that moment, the timestamp, and the source. Mined cards are ordinary Kaiteyo cards: they enter the SRS queue, statistics, and exams like any other.

Mining sources include subtitles, the learning browser, OCR captures, clipboard text, and the text hook for games and texthookers. OCR needs a local Tesseract install. See the [media documentation](/docs/features/media/) and the [mining guide](/guides/mining-anime/).
