category: Getting started
title: Where can I get help when something doesn't work?
---

Start with the [FAQ](/faq/), the [Documentation](/docs/), and the [troubleshooting documentation](/docs/troubleshooting/). For unresolved issues, open a bug report on [GitHub issues](https://github.com/ValiantZippu/Kaiteyo/issues) with your platform, app version, and steps to reproduce — or ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ValiantZippu/Kaiteyo/discussions). Security issues should be reported privately via the [security page](/security/).
