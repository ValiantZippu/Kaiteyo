The **Media** workspace turns your study materials into a mining source. Open a file and extract sentences, save audio clips, and look up vocabulary as you go.

## Supported media

- **Video** — MP4, MKV, WEBM, and more, with subtitle support.
- **Audio** — MP3, WAV, OGG, and more, with an in-app player (play, pause, stop, speed control).
- **Subtitles** — SRT, VTT, ASS, and SSA, with timestamped cues.
- **Images, PDFs, and text** — documents that display inline for review and selection.

## Working with subtitles

Loading a subtitle track aligns cues with the current playback position. Tap any subtitle line to **mine a sentence** or look up individual words in the dictionary.

## Bookmarks and audio clips

- **Bookmarks** — save a timestamped position with a note so you can return to it later.
- **Audio clips** — capture the current playback range as a reusable clip for review or export.

Everything is persisted under `~/.kaiteyo/media-state.json`, so your place, bookmarks, and clips survive restarts.
