---
title: Creating your first deck
description: A five-minute walkthrough from a blank app to your first review session.
---

## 1. Open the decks screen

When you launch Kaiteyo you land on the dashboard. Open the deck browser from the sidebar to see your (empty) deck list.

## 2. Create a deck

Use the add action to create a new deck. Give it a name — e.g. "N5 kanji" or "Travel phrases". Decks can be renamed or deleted any time; your data stays on your device.

## 3. Add material

You can:

- **Add from the built-in dictionary** — search kanji or vocabulary and add entries directly.
- **Create cards manually** — kanji, kana, or vocabulary cards, with your own meaning.
- **Import** — bring in a deck you exported earlier or received from someone else.

## 4. Set your preferences

Per deck you can configure new-card limits, review intervals, and which card mode is used when learning. Sensible defaults are fine to start.

## 5. Study

Start a session and flip cards, answer multiple choice, or write characters. When you've seen a card, it enters the review schedule — check back tomorrow for your first due reviews.

That's it. The [Documentation](/docs/) covers every screen in more depth, and the [FAQ](/faq/) answers the most common questions.
