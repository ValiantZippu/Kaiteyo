title: Mining sentences

**Mining** is the fastest way to build your deck from content you actually read or hear.

## Where mined words come from

- **Dictionary lookups** — any entry can be mined directly into a card.
- **Media subtitles** — mine a whole subtitle line as the example sentence.
- **The learning browser** — select text on any page and mine the sentence.
- **OCR captures** — recognize text on screen and mine the resulting lines.
- **External tools** — the local API lets GameSentenceMiner and similar tools send mined cards straight into Kaiteyo.

## How a mined card is built

A template fills the card with the target word, reading, definition, example sentence, source, and tags. Kaiteyo ships with sensible defaults and lets you add your own templates.

Cards created from mining go straight into your review queue, complete with the sentence that gives them context — the single biggest factor in whether a new word sticks.
