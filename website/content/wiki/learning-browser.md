The **learning browser** lets you read web pages and text files without leaving the app, so every sentence can become study material.

## Reading modes

- **Browser render** — a real rendering of the page with tabs, back/forward, refresh, and bookmarking.
- **Reader mode** — strips clutter and shows the core article text cleanly.
- **Raw text** — shows the extracted plain text for dense reading or copy-out.

## Tabs and history

Open multiple tabs, jump between them, and pick up where you left off. Browsing history and bookmarks are persisted so your reading list survives restarts.

## Mining while you read

Select any text on a page and **look it up** in the dictionary or **mine the sentence** into a card. Reading fluency and vocabulary growth happen at the same time.
